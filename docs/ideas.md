# Ideas

- Compare the reader with a PDF alone and a PDF with an AI summary. Measure how accurately and quickly readers can explain a conclusion, find its evidence, recover a qualification, and identify an unsupported step.
- Let readers annotate or correct a map and save or export their work, with reader annotations visually distinct from the paper and the generated map.
- Support figure-panel references so readers can go directly from a map record to the figure it cites.
- Add PDF text extraction and OCR for papers without usable arXiv HTML. Explore local PDF import and packaging the reader as a plugin.
- Extend the map audit to scan a library, filter by paper and map, and show a compact terminal summary.
- Explore cross-paper synthesis once the reader is useful for understanding a single paper.
