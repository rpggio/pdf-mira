# Sample credits

The Library seeds an empty installation from `papers/`. These samples also support the reader tests and help illustrations.

- Joel Chan, Matthew Akamatsu, David Vargas, Lukas Kawerau, and Michael Gartner, *Steps Towards an Infrastructure for Scholarly Synthesis*, [arXiv:2407.20666v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20666v2), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The sample includes the arXiv PDF, Markdown converted from the HTML, and an AI-generated MIRA map.
- Chris H. J. Hartgerink and Stephen L. George, *Problematic trial detection in ClinicalTrials.gov*, Research Ideas and Outcomes 1, e7462 (2015), [publisher article](https://doi.org/10.3897/rio.1.e7462), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The two hand-authored maps demonstrate repeated, cross-page, approximate, and unquoted records.
- *Measuring Runoff from Rain Gardens* is a fictional study with a hand-authored map. The two-column PDF comes from [the sample source](../scripts/rain-gardens.source.txt).

These files are not covered by the project's MIT license. Reuse them under the licenses above and keep the attribution. The AI-generated maps are derived from the papers they describe.
